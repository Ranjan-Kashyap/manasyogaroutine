Kesha Your Guide And Spiritual Path Finder

Your Guide And Spiritual Path Finder

Meet Kesha – Your Guide On This Journey

Kesha is a yoga coach who has practiced this ancient science for the past 15 years and will guide you on this journey. Her long experience — both in her own practice and in working with people — has helped her design a unique path to learning yoga in a very different way.

“After realizing the miraculous results of this ancient wisdom, I felt that everyone must learn it. It can help you live a healthy and happy long life, mostly free of disease and unwanted health issues.”

She saw many people practicing yoga incorrectly — misunderstanding techniques and practicing in ways that waste time and energy, and sometimes even pose health risks. So she decided to help through yoga consultation: teaching, checking, and correcting the practices you are doing, and guiding you further according to her wisdom and experience.
