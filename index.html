The Foundation of Authentic Yoga

The Eight Limbs of Yoga

Yoga is far more than postures. Explore each limb in a practical, approachable way as you build a lasting practice with Manas Yoga Routine.

1

Yama

Ethical principles that guide how we interact with the world.

2

Niyama

Personal disciplines that nurture growth and self-awareness.

3

Asana

Physical postures that develop strength, flexibility, and steadiness.

4

Pranayama

Breathing practices that calm the mind and improve energy flow.

5

Pratyahara

Gently withdrawing the senses from external distraction.

6

Dharana

Developing concentration toward a single point of focus.

7

Dhyana

A deeper meditative state bringing clarity and calm.

8

Samadhi

Unity, peace, and spiritual fulfillment in yoga.
